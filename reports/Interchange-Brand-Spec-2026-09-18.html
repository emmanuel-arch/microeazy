Screen

Sizes are CSS pixels at 1×. Every raster is served at 2× or better and scaled down, because a mark that is served at its display size is a mark that looks soft on every phone sold since 2015.

What is still missing

The three supplied files cover everything built so far. These are the gaps that will be felt later, in the order they will be felt.

MissingWhere it will be neededWhy it cannot be derived
SVG master of the markAny size above 512px; embroidery; signage; email The supplied PNG has gradients and soft joins. Tracing it would be a redrawing, not a conversion, and the result would not be the same mark.
White / knockout lockupDark decks, the dark report cover, merchandise The wordmark is navy. Recolouring a raster leaves haloes on the anti-aliased edges. Until it exists, dark surfaces use the mark plus live type, which is what they do today.
Single-colour black lockupFax, stamps, one-colour print, legal filings Flattening two hues to one needs a designer's judgement about which shapes survive.
ODPC registration numberEvery report footer and the privacy notice Not supplied, so it does not print. A plausible-looking number on a regulated document would be a fabrication.
